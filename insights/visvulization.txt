Key Findings

• Movies dominate Netflix's catalog.
• Content additions increased significantly after 2015.
• The United States and India are major content contributors.
• Drama and International Movies are among the most common genres.
• Most content is relatively recent.
• Movie duration shows little relationship with cast size.
• Multi-genre titles tend to have slightly larger casts.
• Most numerical features exhibit weak correlations.
